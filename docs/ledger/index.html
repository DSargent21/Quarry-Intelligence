The Method

Four rules, carved once.

Every prior series learned the same lesson: edges die from tinkering. Jade is governed by four fixed rules and a public referee.

I

The Skill Gate

A capper’s lifetime record, shrunk toward the market’s −5% prior (k = 24), must clear a .55 win rate before any pick is considered. Hot streaks don’t clear it. Proven edge does.

II

The Band

Only prices between 1.80 and 2.00 decimal (−125 to −100). Blind money loses in every band to the vig; this is where the model’s skill selection converts best.

III

The Cap

Five orders a day, flat one unit, no parlays, no chasing, no doubling after losses. Volume is capped by rule, not by mood — the grade rewards discipline, not action.

IV

The Freeze

The model was frozen before the ledger opened. When it is wrong, the ledger says so in public. Nothing is re-tuned to make a chart look better — that is the whole point.

The Record

Latest verdicts.

Graded entries, most recent first. Losses are printed in the same ink as wins — that is the arrangement.

The Risk Page

What could break it.

One clean month. September is a single holdout; its 90% confidence interval spans −4.5% to +19.7%. The forward ledger — 100 graded picks — is the real experiment.

Prices at the whistle. Backtest odds are recorded at grading time. If prices move between posting and grading, part of the measured edge may not be executable at bet time.

Concentration. 41% of September profit came from one capper. Cappers go cold; the daily cap and skill gate are the containment, not a cure.

Drawdown. Worst observed Sep drawdown: -1.7u. Live variance will be worse. Stake sizes should assume a 15–20u drawdown is normal.

Market drift. The model is frozen between retrains. If the market adapts, the ledger decays in public view — by design, before stakes grow.

This is not advice. No picks are sold here. This is a public research experiment on disclosed data, staked at sizes that cannot hurt.
